# JOKES.md

Five jokes, in no particular order of quality but strictly numbered anyway.

## 1. Recursion

To understand recursion, you must first understand recursion.

## 2. The optimist and the ray tracer

A ray tracer walks into a bar. It bounces off the counter, off a mirror, off the
ceiling, off a stranger's glasses, and after fifty bounces it gives up and
returns black.

## 3. Two hard problems

There are only two hard things in computer science: cache invalidation, naming
things, and off-by-one errors.

## 4. Precision

A programmer's spouse says: "Go to the store and buy a loaf of bread. If they
have eggs, buy a dozen."

The programmer comes back with twelve loaves of bread.

## 5. Concurrency

Knock knock.

Race condition.

Who's there?
