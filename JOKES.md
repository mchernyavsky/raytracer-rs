# Jokes

Five favorites, with a bias toward the kind you'd tell while waiting for a render.

1. **Why did the ray tracer refuse to gossip?**
   It only reflected on what it saw directly.

2. A programmer's spouse says: "Go to the store and buy a loaf of bread. If they have eggs, get a dozen."
   The programmer comes home with twelve loaves of bread.

3. **How many programmers does it take to change a light bulb?**
   None — that's a hardware problem.

4. There are two hard things in computer science: cache invalidation, naming things, and off-by-one errors.

5. **Why do photons never check luggage?**
   They're travelling light.
